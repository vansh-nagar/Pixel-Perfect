My projects tend to end up in someone’s hands: a concert programme, a folded map, a book with a well-used spine. I design identities and publications, then help them find their way onto the web.

Below is a selection of recent commissions. I also keep process notes on the decisions that rarely make it into the final presentation. For new work, send a brief.

Recent commissions

2026✳After the EncoreFestival identity

An identity for a three-night chamber music festival. The same modular lettering runs across street posters, stage schedules, and a website visitors can check while waiting in line.

2026◌A Fold in the RiverWalking map

A pocket guide commissioned by a riverside arts group. I walked the route with the organizers before designing a map that folds to show one stretch at a time.

2025⊞Small EditionsPublisher website

A catalogue for an independent poetry press. Each book gets space for a sample spread, a short extract, and the details a reader would check in a bookshop.

2025⌁Room for a Second ChairExhibition graphics

Labels and a printed guide for an exhibition of repaired furniture. The captions separate the original maker from the person who restored each piece.

In the studio

I share a studio in Ahmedabad with a bookbinder. There is usually a paper sample on the windowsill and a print drying on the only spare chair. I work directly with editors, organizers, and small cultural teams.

Process notes

Print it at the size people will holdJuly 2026

A map that felt generous on screen became crowded when folded. Printing the first draft early changed the route labels, the key, and where we put the emergency contact.

The back cover has a job tooMarch 2026

For a programme people carry between venues, the back cover is the quickest place to find the next address. We moved the sponsor credits inside and gave that surface to the schedule.
